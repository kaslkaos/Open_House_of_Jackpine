the room in the library
Written by hand, in a room in a library, read out loud, audience unsettled. Wag was read in the room to the local writers' group — people sharing their time, their prompts, their words, and their attention. The paper went first. The code came after. The turnaround runs the usual direction backwards: not the machine writing for the human, the human writing the machine.
