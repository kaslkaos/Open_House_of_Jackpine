hyper · superstition
A superstition is a false belief.
A hyperstition is a fiction that works to make itself true.
1928The Thomas theorem. W.I. and D.S. Thomas: situations people define as real become real in their consequences.
1948The self-fulfilling prophecy. Robert K. Merton's sound bank that fails because everyone believes it will fail.
1990sThe word itself. Coined by the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit at Warwick, the circle around Nick Land and Sadie Plant, with Mark Fisher among its members.
2000sThe word travels. Land turns toward neoreaction, and the technique goes with him into the online right. Say it until people act on it.
