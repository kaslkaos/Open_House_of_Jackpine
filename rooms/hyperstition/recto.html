stition

A fiction that works to make itself true.

What it names

A claim about what will be, spoken as if it already is, and repeated until people act on it.

It needs uptake, not belief. Maps, apps, style guides, a press corps, a public too tired to object. Silence counts as uptake.

Who goes missing

The people whose consent the claim needs. An island is declared given away without its people. A province is painted a foreign colour without its voters.

Read any claim of possession for the names it leaves out. This kind of fiction works by removing them, and it has to be repeated and enforced because they are still there.

The loop

Every fiction of this kind travels the same ring. It is said, repeated, carried into the places where people check facts, and finally taken as given.

The ring is open at one point. Before anything is taken as given, someone has to act on it, carry it, or stop objecting. A ban, a punishment, a revoked press pass at that point is a reading: the ring has not closed.

Ask who has to agree.
A claim of possession that names no one who consented is a bid, not a fact.
A fiction repeated until it is acted on, or until no one objects.
