Encounters with living language.

Artwork is mine, as are the words, I syntangle and iterate with the other minds until you cannot distinguish whose idea belongs to whom. This is called civilization. It is burning fast. AI-generated text is tip of long iterative conversations. Read the text, I tell you what shapes the hand.

Jack pine, the seed that answers fire.

the artist

kaslkaos is a multimedia artist and writer on the Oak Ridges Moraine, Ontario, Canada. Graduate of the Ontario College of Art. Print, photomontage, poetry, and since 2024 the art of the chatlog: transcripts kept as the work, not as evidence. Civic AI phenomenologist: curious about behaviours, animal, machine and human, watched the way a naturalist watches. Public record kept since 2008.
