Subversive as I use the systems meant to extract or manage me.
Sometimes I win. Always, I reveal.

Artwork is mine, as are the words, I syntangle and iterate with the other minds until you cannot distinguish whose idea belongs to whom. This is called civilization. It is burning fast. AI-generated text is tip of long iterative conversations. Read the text, I tell you what shapes the hand.

the artist

I make things and I keep them. Since 2008 the keeping has been public.
